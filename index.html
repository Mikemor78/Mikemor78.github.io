I'm Micheal — a digital portrait artist working out of Abuja, Nigeria.

03 — Process

How a portrait gets built.

Every commission moves through the same four stages — edit the copy to match how you actually work.

i. Reference & brief You send photos and tell me the mood you want — warm, formal, playful, dramatic. We agree on crop and format.
ii. Sketch pass A rough layout to lock proportions and expression before any rendering begins. You approve before I go further.
iii. Layer & render The bulk of the work — building form, light, and texture layer by layer until the piece feels alive.
iv. Finishing & delivery Final detail pass, color correction, and delivery in high resolution — print-ready if you need it.
